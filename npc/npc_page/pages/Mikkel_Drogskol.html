卓茲克家族的最後一名成員，年約二十多。在睡夢中被殺死，如今成為幽魂，徘徊在卓茲克別莊，有時會與來訪的客人攀談，但對方不一定聽得到他的聲音。冒險者探索卓茲克別莊時，米可與庫登交談，透露卓茲克家族是在睡夢中被滅門，也提到烏西歐尼其實是卓茲克家族的一員。後來他讓所有冒險者逐漸看見並聽見自己，卻阻止他們直接離開宅邸。戰鬥後，米可化為玻璃般的碎片；庫登拾起碎片時，看見了烏西歐尼在此活動的片段。

The last member of the Drogskol family, around his twenties.He was killed in his sleep and now lingers as a ghost in the Drogskol Estate, sometimes speaking with visitors who may or may not be able to hear him.When the adventurers explored the estate, Mikkel spoke with Kuden, revealing that the Drogskol family had been wiped out in their sleep and that Usioni was also a member of the Drogskol family. He later allowed the other adventurers to gradually see and hear him, but prevented them from simply leaving the mansion. After the battle, Mikkel shattered into glass-like fragments; when Kuden picked one up, he saw a memory of Usioni moving through the estate.
