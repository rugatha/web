被稱為蜘蛛王。是多數人認定蜘蛛神教的主神，但其實地位低於母親大人。10836年，由妖精王國的艾蒙醫生、法倫主教，和諾威爾的屍體粉塵，透過儀式融合而成。冒險者在麥克嵩王宮圖書館地下特殊館藏中得知，阿爾芬諾並非單一妖精化身，而是由一群妖精對人類的恨意灌入單一妖精軀體後形成的集體意志。阿爾芬諾曾透過圖書館員與圖伊特、伊努進行心靈對話，展現強大的力量；對話結束時，圖書館員也被吸成乾屍。

Known as the Spider King.Most people regard Alfenor as the main god of the Spider God religion, but his standing is actually below The Mother.In 10836, Alfenor was created through a ritual that fused Dr. Aemon, Bishop Falon, and the powdered corpse of Norwell from the elven kingdom.In the restricted collections beneath the Macksohn royal library, the adventurers learned that Alfenor was not a single elf incarnation, but a collective will born when the hatred of many elves toward humans was poured into one elven body.Alfenor once used a librarian to communicate telepathically with Tweeter and Inu, displaying immense power; when the conversation ended, the librarian had been drained into a dried corpse.
