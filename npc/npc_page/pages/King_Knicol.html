地底王尼可大人。冒險者於地下城與哥布林激戰後俘虜的一隻哥布林。在哥布林王戰死後，被冒險者加冕為地底王，並賜名尼可；本名呱啦。尼可大多數時間被烏犧奧泥五花大綁掛在背後，作為防身工具。後來在占卜老太太施法後，他變得乖順，像活生生的魁儡國王。安納戴克加入冒險時自稱受神王尼可與烏犧奧泥大法師指派。後來安納戴克身上掛著「神王的指引」，也就是神王尼可的手指項鍊；米多莉曾以定位魔法追蹤它到麥克嵩西北方的朗甫頓。

Lord Knicol, King of the Underground.A goblin captured by the adventurers after a battle in the dungeon. After the Goblin King died, the adventurers crowned him King of the Underground and named him Knicol; his birth name was Guala.Knicol spent most of his time tied to Usioni's back as a defensive tool. After a fortune-telling old woman cast magic on him, he became obedient, like a living puppet king.When Anedak joined the adventurers, he claimed to have been sent by God-King Knicol and Archmage Usioni. Later, Anedak carried the Guidance of the God-King: a necklace made from King Knicol's finger. Midori once tracked it with magic to Rumpton, northwest of Macksohn.
