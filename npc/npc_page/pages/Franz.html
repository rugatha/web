荒野旋律在柏納斯家晚宴認識的吟遊詩人，年輕人類男性。他表示自己也曾和冒險者們到處遊歷、搜集故事，對《沃瓦倫之歌》很感興趣，邀請荒野旋律談談這首歌背後的故事。在麥克嵩期間，居住於柏納斯家附近旅店。冒險者在他的客房發現法蘭茲真實身份是暗影追蹤者的前成員：年輕時，被稱為「湛藍法朗茲」的他曾與尼巴隆、史瓦茲、達里歐一起調查暗影之父，之後也學會拉索斯語。法蘭茲曾引發暗影之父現身襲擊，但也在之後加入冒險者，跟隨眾人穿越妖精荒野。回到物質世界後，他沒有與隊伍繼續同行。

A young human bard whom Rhythm of the Wild met at the Berners family banquet.He said he had also traveled and collected stories with other adventurers, showed interest in The Song of Vowalon, and invited Rhythm of the Wild to discuss the story behind it.While in Macksohn, he stayed at an inn near the Berners household. In his room, the adventurers discovered that Franz was actually a former Shadow Tracker: when he was younger, he, with the name "Franz the Blue", had investigated the Father of Shadows alongside Nebalon, Schwartz, and Dario, and later learned the language Laxthosian.Franz once caused the Father of Shadows to manifest and attack, but later joined the adventurers and traveled with them through the Feywild. After returning to the material world, he did not continue with the party.
