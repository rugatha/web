Marsh the Willow

雷亞的師父，居住在風拂山丘。馬許神情慵懶、略顯不耐，卻擁有連瑪萊雅將軍都十分忌憚的強大力量。多年前，馬許與拉比曾在林中的白色小教堂為雷亞的父母阿哈努・席弗斯頓與克萊芬的蕾絲塔證婚，也為他們埋葬；雷亞也自小在他的指導下於風拂山丘修煉。瑪萊雅將軍率軍摧毀小教堂並包圍中途之家時，馬許撕開一道通往風拂山丘的黑暗裂縫現身。他輕易擊飛試圖追趕的士兵，將雷亞與同伴們帶離戰場，並要他們回去繼續修煉。

The master of Leah, Marsh lives in the Windblown Hills. He carries himself with a lazy, faintly irritated air, yet possesses power formidable enough to make even General Maliya extremely wary.Years ago, Marsh and Ravi officiated the wedding of Leah's parents, Ahanu Silverstone and Lesta of Clyphen, at the small white chapel in the woods, and also buried them. Leah also trained under Marsh in the Windblown Hills from childhood.When General Maliya's army destroyed the chapel and surrounded the Halfway Home, Marsh appeared through a dark fissure leading to the Windblown Hills. He effortlessly knocked away a pursuing soldier, brought Leah and her companions out of the battle, and ordered them to return with him for more training.
