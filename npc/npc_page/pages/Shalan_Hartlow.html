方努爾學術界的重要人物，也是茜卓・派松博士的熟人。他輕鬆准許冒險者進入限制書庫，知道茜卓・派松博士的脾氣很快會冷靜下來，並透露方努爾傳送門與代號「狐狸」的滲透者相關。茜卓・派松博士身亡後，他將茜卓・派松博士在方努爾的房產轉到YHWH名下。

An important academic figure in Fanaur and an acquaintance of Dr. Chandra Pyresong. He casually granted the adventurers access to the restricted library, knew Chandra would calm down quickly, and revealed information about the Fanaur portal and an infiltrator known as Fox. After Chandra’s death, he transferred Chandra’s Fanaur property to YHWH.
