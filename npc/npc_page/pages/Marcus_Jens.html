麥克嵩的貴族，家族從事情報搜集。雇用了一名叫伊的貧民窟少年（伊稱呼他為M伯爵），似乎在調查和蜘蛛神教相關的事。有一名叫瑪莉安的妹妹被關在王宮監獄內，冒險者受他委託，將瑪莉安從監獄救出來。多年前曾購買了大量的魔法球，魔法球的功能似乎非常多元，而且不是只有他自己能使用。馬可斯是月神教的信徒。在麥克嵩的擁王派失利後，與冒險者們一同逃往洛森王國，前往尋找母親大人的旅途。然而，在一次與月光狩獵會的戰鬥中，被敵人的詛咒殺死。冒險者將他安葬，並立了一個木製的墓碑。

A Macksohn noble whose family specializes in intelligence gathering.Marcus hired a young man from the old town named Yi, who called him Count M, to collect information about The Spider Religion.His sister Marianne was imprisoned in the royal prison, and Marcus commissioned the adventurers to rescue her.Years earlier, he purchased a large number of magical spheres with many functions, and he did not seem to be their only user.Marcus was a follower of the moon god.After the pro-king faction lost in Macksohn, he fled with the adventurers to the Lothum Kingdom to search for The Mother. During a battle with the Moonlight Hunters, he was killed by a curse. The adventurers buried him and marked his grave with a wooden marker.
