麥克嵩當地的七歲小女孩。父母在比武大會中死去，因此成為孤兒。因為視台北市議員為偶像，要求加入一起冒險。在米多莉與喜利的認可下加入台北市議員，並視米多莉為媽媽，喜利為爸爸。說話十分衝動不忌言，才剛加入台北市議員，得罪了當中不少人。到妖精荒野後，以救世主的身份留在那裡，但最終除了波外，其他當地居民都在拉索斯教入侵後被消滅。回到物質世界時已年過40，便和冒險者們一起繼續以剿滅拉索斯教為目標冒險。

A seven-year-old girl from Macksohn whose parents died in the Macksohn Tournament, leaving her an orphan.Beau idolized the Councilors of Taipei and asked to join them. With Midori and Siri's approval, she joined the party and came to see Midori as her mother and Siri as her father.She speaks recklessly and offended several party members soon after joining.After traveling to the Feywild, Beau stayed there as the Savior. When the Laxthos invasion destroyed the local civilization, she was the only survivor. Because of the Feywild's time distortion, she returned to the material world in her forties and continued traveling with the adventurers to fight Laxthos.
