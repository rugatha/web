麥克嵩城門旅店的老闆。是貴族家庭的後代，但為了證明自己可以自力更生，開了城門旅店。個性十分單純，容易相信人。對米多莉有所迷戀，為了向米多莉賠罪，曾送他一條家中的珍珠項鍊。兩年後台北市議員重返麥克嵩時，凱文仍經營城門旅店；他見到米多莉後反應強烈，又因聽見波稱米多莉為媽媽而心碎。當冒險者調查空洞的蘿琳時，凱文表示自己幾個月前仍見過蘿琳，但已快兩年沒和他說話。

The owner of the Gates Inn in Macksohn. He came from a noble family, but opened the inn to prove that he could live independently.Kevin is naive and trusts people easily. He had a crush on Midori, and once gave her a pearl necklace from his family as an apology.Two years later, when the Councilors of Taipei returned to Macksohn, Kevin was still running the Gates Inn. He reacted strongly on seeing Midori again, and was heartbroken when he heard Beau call Midori her mother. When the adventurers investigated the hollow-seeming Roline, Kevin said he had still seen Roline a few months earlier, but had not spoken with her for almost two years.
