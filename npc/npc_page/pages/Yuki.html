對米多莉而言非常重要的人，多次以幻影出現在米多莉的腦中，也曾在妖精荒野時被艾爾比以幻象呈現在大家面前。過去似乎曾被關押在一個伸手不見五指的地方。在聖拉雅米島的洞穴，被發現操控著拉茲特獸攻擊老鼠人；在金海岸礦坑事件中，冒險者取得的紀錄顯示他與拉索斯教的實驗與滿月儀式有關，且與米多莉可能站在對立面。

A very important figure to Midori, who appeared in Midori's mind many times and was also shown to the party as an illusion by Elby in the Feywild.She seems to have once been imprisoned in a place of complete darkness.On St. Rayami Island, Yuki was found controlling a Laztek to attack ratfolk. During the Gold Coast Mines incident, records recovered by the adventurers tied her to Laxthos experiments and a full-moon ritual, suggesting that she and Midori may now stand on opposing sides.
