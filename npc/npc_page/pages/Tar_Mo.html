尖頂寺出身的半妖精武僧兼聖騎士，信仰拉索斯，是龍恩年幼時帶領他的師兄。外貌俊俏而風霜漸重，常帶著難以讀懂的微笑；左臂在肘下截斷，僅餘右手。依塔爾莫本人所述，他離開尖頂寺後曾風光一時，卻因得罪派系而四處逃亡，最後受與尖頂寺有關的拉索斯教會收留，躲進偏僻的「灰祠」。看守一隻受神父與修女保護的乾枯手臂時，他一時衝動斬斷自己的手臂，卻無法將其接上；之後偷走手臂，逃往妖精荒野。在妖精荒野的中心樹前，他遇見渴求金屬手臂的龍恩，認定師弟比自己更像「被選中的人」，並將那隻手臂交給龍恩。他返回物質世界的旅程比預期多耗了數年，部分妖精荒野的記憶也無法找回。他在尖頂寺與龍恩重逢，任由師弟毆打自己，仍微笑著詢問那隻變異手臂是否好用。他以拉索斯語重述「本該屬於你的東西，終究會回到你身邊」，隨後開啟黑色漩渦，帶龍恩到安靜處交談，表明願意協助他。他協助龍恩在尖頂寺尋找眼睛，嘗試清除僧人的記憶或施加蠱惑，再放走他們，暗中監看住持的動向。他主張藉恐慌逼寺方替他們找出眼睛，並告訴龍恩，得到眼睛後將有足以對抗世界的力量。蒂雅菈長老前來通報閉門儀式與住持的計畫。當她表示受詛咒所限、不能洩露找尋眼睛的方法時，塔爾莫以彎刀抵住她的喉嚨，將最後的決定交給龍恩。

A half-elf monk and paladin from Peak Temple who worships Laxthos. Tar'Mo was the senior who guided Ron as a young novice. Once strikingly handsome, he now bears the marks of a harder life and often wears an unreadable smile. His left arm is amputated below the elbow; only his right hand remains.According to Tar'Mo, he enjoyed a period of success after leaving Peak Temple, but offended powerful factions and became a fugitive. A Laxthos church connected to the temple eventually sheltered him in the remote Grey Shrine. While guarding a withered arm protected by its priests and nuns, he impulsively severed his own arm, only to find that he could not attach the relic. He later stole it and fled to the Feywild.At the central tree in the Feywild, he encountered Ron pleading for a metal arm. Convinced that his junior was more likely than he was to be a “chosen one,” he gave the arm to Ron. His return to the Material Realm took several years longer than expected, and parts of his memories of the Feywild remain inaccessible.he reunited with Ron at Peak Temple. He let his junior punch him, still smiling as he asked how the altered arm was working. Repeating the Laxthosian words “What was meant to be yours will find its way back to you in the end,” he opened a black vortex, led Ron somewhere quiet to talk, and offered his help.he helped Ron search Peak Temple for the eye. He tried wiping monks' memories or charming them before releasing them to watch the abbot's movements. His plan was to create panic and make the temple find the eye for them. He told Ron that possessing it would give him the power to take on the world.Elder Tiara brought news of the rite of seclusion and the abbot's plan. When she said a curse prevented her from revealing how the eye would be found, Tar'Mo held a curved blade to her throat and left the final decision to Ron.
