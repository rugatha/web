過去由溫德家飼養的巴哥犬，現在與布朗一起冒險。似乎能透過心靈感應的方式與人溝通，甚至可以化成似人的生物或野狼。對溫德大人似乎懷有很深的敵意，但對彼得則是非常保護。在布朗離開波馬贊後跟隨他前往魏斯科與夏庭森。途中，瑪芬先生曾變成野狼撲倒馬匹，證實布朗先前在波馬贊見到的野狼正是他。之後，瑪芬先生似乎失去與布朗溝通的能力，只剩下普通犬隻般的反應；即使戴著羅納德給他的魔術師帽，也只會吠叫與抗拒布朗修剪指甲。

A pug formerly kept by the Wind family, now adventuring with Brown.Mr. Muffins seems able to communicate telepathically and can even transform into a humanoid being or a wolf.He appears to harbor deep hostility toward Lord Wind, but is very protective of Peter.After Brown left Bomozan, Mr. Muffins followed him to Wescoe and Shartingson. On the road, he transformed into a wolf and pounced on Brown's horse, confirming that the wolf Brown had seen in Bomozan was indeed him.Later, Mr. Muffins seemed to lose the ability to communicate with Brown and behaved more like an ordinary dog; even while wearing the magician's hat Ronald gave him, he only barked and resisted when Brown tried to trim his claws.
