台北市議員等人在麥克嵩買的馬。十分年輕健壯，適合拉馬車或快速奔跑。在多侖維爾時曾被竊馬賊騎走，後來被冒險者們攔下牽回。雷米爆炸身亡後，斯比迪一度受驚想往前衝，嚇到了正在啃食肉塊的酷爆。後來薩梅爾短暫回到一般世界時，發現斯比迪和馬車都不見了。

A horse the party bought in Macksohn.Speedy is young and strong, suited for pulling a carriage or running fast.In Dorrenville, a horse thief once rode Speedy away before the adventurers stopped him and brought Speedy back. After Lemmy died in an explosion, Speedy was startled and tried to rush forward, frightening Kubao while Kubao was eating pieces of flesh. Later, when Samael briefly returned to the material world, Speedy and the carriage were both gone.
