由妖精王國的能量產出的蛋，孵出的魔法生物。其外型由照養他的洛特的意識所塑型。擁有超出想像的知識，但有時候會突然說出讓人恐懼又無法理解的話語。與洛特一起在母親大人的屠殺中生存。與洛特一起來到斐納札後消失了一段時間，最終在烏波成為心空席後，以巨蛇的型態現身，且有著痛苦人臉的花紋與殘肢長在身上。芒果將賽柏咬傷，將他拖入傳送門消失。後來派琶告知冒險者，常翠林已擊退芒果並救出賽柏。芒果最終帶著四隻蛇妖襲擊常翠林總部，被涅西斯藉由派琶喚出的樹木困住，並在賽柏的致命一擊下死去。

A magical creature hatched from an egg born of Feywild energy.Its form was shaped by the consciousness of Lott, who cared for it.Mango possesses knowledge far beyond expectation, but sometimes says frightening and incomprehensible things.Mango survived The Mother's massacre together with Lott.After arriving in Fenadra with Lott, Mango disappeared for a time. After Ubbo became the Seat of Hollow Heart, Mango reappeared as a giant serpent, patterned with anguished human faces and bearing severed limbs on its body.Mango bit Psyber and dragged him through a portal before vanishing. Py'Par later told the adventurers that the Evergreen had driven Mango off and rescued Psyber.Mango ultimately attacked the Evergreen headquarters with four snake fiends, was trapped by trees called forth by Nessis through Py'Par, and died under Psyber's fatal strike.
