It was labeled BoBo-OMEGA.It had a setting that due to its name close to the robot series BoBo, it disliked its name when it was young.There seemed to be someone behind it other that Queen Uma of the Chryomen Kingdom, but it remained anonymous.Bol-Bol is fully aware that it is actually a robot.
