瑟巴諾當地的黑幫老大之一，身材十分壯碩，但是手下似乎都不太擅長打架。鎮上的一間酒館「霍布的派對屋」似乎是他的黑幫集散地。似乎是拉索斯教的信奉者，手下的身上有刺拉索斯語的祝禱文，與德雷爾過去就認識。和冒險者們談了交易，只要他們將鎮長家的魔法盒交給他，就能贖回被他綁走的喬納森。真身是來自深淵的一頭綁匪怪蛙，其手下為魚人，與拉索斯教簽訂了契約。他計劃在瑟巴諾建造某種偉大的裝置，卻被冒險者們打斷，也失去大多數手下。在搶到魔法盒子後，霍布利用盒子與黏液製造出冒險者們的複製體，將多名冒險者溶成泥後消失。

One of Sebano's local gang bosses, very large and strong, though his men did not seem very capable.His tavern, Hobb's Party House, served as a gathering place for his gang.He appeared to be a follower of Laxthos; his men bore Laxthosian prayers tattooed on their bodies, and he seemed to have known Drayer from before.Hobb struck a deal with the adventurers: if they brought him the magic box from the mayor's house, he would release the kidnapped Jonathan.His true form was a kidnapper frog-like creature from the Deep, served by fishmen and bound by contract to Laxthos. He planned to build a great device in Sebano, but the adventurers disrupted the plan and killed most of his workers.After seizing the magic box, Hobb used it and slime to create copies of the adventurers, melted several of them into mud, and vanished.
