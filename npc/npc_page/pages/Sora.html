卡什拉鎮居民，被匿名之聲的領袖萊斯麗與瓦克松醫師利用作為儀式材料而死亡；他的父親彼特之後一直與他的屍體同住。在746年的卡什拉幻象或記憶中，索拉依照瓦克松醫師的預言等待冒險者，介紹嘟嘟與瓦萊羅給眾人認識，也逐漸想起自己早已死去；真相揭露後，他在眾人面前化為塵土。冒險者後來在彼特家床底找到他與嘟嘟的屍體，並得知始祖拉茲特獸曾抽乾他的靈魂。

A resident of Kashla who was used as ritual material by Leslie Grunfeld, leader of the Anonymous Voice, and Dr. Vaxon. His father Pete later lived with his corpse.In the Kashla illusion or memory of year 746, Sora waited for the adventurers according to Dr. Vaxon’s prophecy, introduced Dudu and Valero, and gradually remembered that he had already died; when the truth surfaced, he dissolved into dust before them.The adventurers later found his and Dudu’s bodies under Pete’s bed, and learned that the Progenitor Laztek had drained his soul.
