A paladin of Keinra with short silver-white hair, and the adventuring partner of the half-elf Ralf.The two were invited by Captain Marin to St. Rayami Island to help investigate the disappearances and poisoning incidents there. After Ralf went missing during the investigation, Liliana sought the adventurers' help and helped push the investigation toward the Gold Coast Mines.
