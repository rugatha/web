斐納札的機器人衛兵，十分有群眾魅力。與烏波一同爭取心空席的席次，並順利晉級到決選。在決賽中，魯米歐多次重創烏波，幾乎殺死他；最後在冒險夥伴們介入下被擊敗，被賽博的釘頭錘擊飛，在空中化為火球後墜地爆炸。冒險者回到靈魂議會後，魯米歐沒有再出現。

A charismatic warforged guard in Fenadra.Lumio ran for the vacancy of the Seat of Hollow Heart against Ubbo and reached the final round.In the final contest, Lumio repeatedly devastated Ubbo and nearly killed him. After the other adventurers intervened, Psyber's mace launched Lumio into the air; he turned into a fireball and exploded when he hit the ground. When the adventurers returned to the Soul Parliament, Lumio did not appear again.
