瑟巴諾鎮長索恩的妻子，視覺年齡約二十初頭，是個妖精。在索恩當上鎮長一段時間後來到瑟巴諾，與索恩相識並結婚。在瑟巴諾被離奇屠村後，出現在帕拉丁面前，並透過自己的技術，將溶成泥的冒險者們重新培育出來。似乎是蜘蛛王阿爾芬諾的後裔以及信徒。在鎮長家的地底有座自己的實驗室。將冒險者們重新培育出來後，安娜便消失了，但冒險者們卻發現自己身上出現了蜘蛛網型的印記，似乎是個詛咒。再次出現時，安娜與母親大人一同威脅帕拉丁，後來又偽裝成蘿琳試著將帕拉丁帶走，實際是想對帕拉丁逼婚，以藉由他的貴族身份拿點好處。透過帕拉丁的身體，將母親大人奪舍後，成為新的蜘蛛神。

Anna is an elf who is the wife of mayor Thorne of Sebano, and looks like in her early 20s. She came to Sebano short after Thorne became the mayor of the town, and got married in a short time.After the macabre slaughter of Sebano, Anna showed up to Paladin, and reformed the other adventurers from mud.Anna seems to be a descendant and believer of Alfenor.She has her own lab at the basement of the mayor’s house.After recreating the adventurers, Anna disappeared, but the adventurers found some spider web-shaped totem on their bodies, that seems like a curse.When Anna reappeared, she and The Mother threatened Paladin, and also disguised as Roline, trying to take Paladin away. She was intended to force Paladin into marriage to take advantage of his noble identity.Anna later invaded The Mother’s body through Paladin, and became the new Spider God.
