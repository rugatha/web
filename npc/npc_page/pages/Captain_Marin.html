方努爾市的一名船長。受羅賓遜主教委託，載冒險者們到南方的荒島，尋找《雪の化身の目覚め》的下落。多年後，聖拉雅米島成為她的封地；她接獲島上居民失蹤與中毒的消息後，帶新的冒險者登島調查，並請他們將調查報告送往聖津菈大教堂。在金海岸礦坑事件後，她提供津菈教協助者勳章，讓冒險者前往方努爾回報拉索斯教與拉巴比石的線索。

A captain from Fanaur.Commissioned by Bishop Robinson to bring adventurers to the southern island to find the book "雪の化身の目覚め." Years later, St. Rayami Island became her fiefdom. After residents began disappearing and suffering unexplained poisonings, she brought new adventurers to investigate and asked them to deliver their report to Saint Keinra Cathedral.After the Gold Coast Mine incident, she gave the adventurers badges identifying them as Keinra's helpers so they could report the Laxthos and Lababi-stone clues in Fanaur.
