拉黎爾的狼夥伴，有比一般狼更高的智慧與力量。異色瞳，左眼藍色，右眼橘色，與拉黎爾形影不離，甚至願意為了保護拉黎爾而死。後來蓋瑞跟隨拉黎爾與羅特等人前往洛森王國。在母親大人的巢穴中，異變的母親大人以「治療」為名用觸手纏住蓋瑞，硬生生折斷他的脊椎，使他死亡。

Ra'Leer's wolf companion, with greater intelligence and strength than an ordinary wolf.Gary had heterochromia: a blue left eye and an orange right eye. He was always with Ra'Leer and would even die to protect her.Gary later accompanied Ra'Leer, Lott, and the others into the Lothum Kingdom. In The Mother's lair, The Mother, in her mutated form, claimed to be healing Gary, wrapped him in tentacles, and snapped his spine, killing him.
