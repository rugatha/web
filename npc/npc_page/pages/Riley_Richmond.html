里奇蒙家族的當家掌門人，巴托斯的女兒，喬里森的信奉者。過去為T.I.D.E.S.方尖碑的成員。冒險者受邀參與T.I.D.E.S.集會時，蘿拉・費許朋向他發起挑戰，直接奪走他的右眼作為信物；萊里因此失去方尖碑席位並被逐出T.I.D.E.S.，連他邀請的冒險者們也被迫離場。被逐出後，萊里帶冒險者前往土城，使用自己在赫洛登瀑布後方的研究中心「渡鴉中樞」，並委託他們取得受喬里森祝福河水漂流的蘋果木枝幹，用來製作讓自己重返T.I.D.E.S.的神秘器具。

The head of the Richmond family, daughter of Bartosz, and a believer of Jeorisan.Riley was once an Obelisk of T.I.D.E.S. When the adventurers attended a T.I.D.E.S. gathering, Laura Fishburne challenged her and took Riley's right eye as the token of victory. Riley lost her Obelisk seat, was expelled from T.I.D.E.S., and even the adventurers she had invited were forced to leave.After her expulsion, Riley took the adventurers to Mudtown and used her research center behind Heroden Falls, the Raven Nexus. She commissioned them to obtain a branch from apple-tree driftwood carried by river water blessed by Jeorisan, intending to craft a mysterious device that could help her return to T.I.D.E.S.
