麥丁頓現任國王艾略特四世的母親，在前國王時期似乎沒有人知道他的存在。被智者奧利佛暗示不是人類，似乎有一定程度的魔法能力，能主動進行心靈溝通。根據瑪莉安的說法，本名為瑪麗蓮，過去是他蜘蛛神教的夥伴，但後來信奉拉索斯教，改名為瑪莉恩，並揭發瑪莉安臥底的身份，使他被關入地牢，因此瑪莉安對他恨之入骨。瑪莉安也認為艾略特四世只是魁儡。後來冒險者發現運送巴納沃二世屍體、皇冠與玉璽的夫妻被刺客射殺，而線索指向瑪莉恩。

The mother of the current King of Mattington, Elliot IV. No one seemed to know of her during the former king's reign.Oliver the Wise hinted that she was not human. She appears to possess magical power, including active telepathy.According to Marianne, Marian was originally named Marilyn and had once been her companion among the followers of the Spider God. She later turned to Laxthos, changed her name to Marian, exposed Marianne as an infiltrator, and caused her imprisonment, earning Marianne's hatred.Marianne also regarded Elliot IV as a puppet. Later, when the adventurers found a couple transporting Benevol II's corpse, crown, and royal seal, the assassins who killed the couple appeared to point back to Marian.
