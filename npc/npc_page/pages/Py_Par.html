涅西斯教團「常翠林」成員之一，在冒險者們追尋芒果的行跡時發現他。派琶告知冒險者們教團已將巨蛇（芒果）擊退，並拯救了被巨蛇綑綁的男人，即冒險者的夥伴賽柏。雖然他踏上的草地都會化為花田，但人似乎有點迷糊，對距離、時間等都沒有很精確的概念。在護送冒險者前往常翠林中繼站時，派琶遭到芬尼爾信徒與蛇身女妖襲擊。雖然受傷，他仍催促眾人趕路，並警告金髮的佛拉可能有危險。抵達常翠林總部後，派琶在芒果與四隻蛇妖的襲擊中保護冒險者與佛拉，數度倒下仍繼續戰鬥。關鍵時刻，涅西斯藉由派琶的身體發聲並召出樹木困住芒果，最終讓賽柏得以給予芒果致命一擊。

A member of the Nessis order known as the Evergreen. The adventurers found Py'Par while tracking Mango.Py'Par told the adventurers that the order had already driven off the giant serpent, Mango, and saved the man Mango had bound: Psyber, the adventurers' companion.Although meadows turn into flower fields wherever Py'Par steps, Py'Par seems somewhat scatterbrained and has an imprecise sense of distance and time.While guiding the adventurers toward an Evergreen relay station, Py'Par was attacked by followers of Phyneal and a gorgon-like foe. Though wounded, Py'Par urged everyone onward and warned that their blond friend, Phola, might be in danger.After reaching the Evergreen headquarters, Py'Par protected the adventurers and Phola during Mango and four snake fiends' attack, falling several times but continuing to fight. At the critical moment, Nessis spoke through Py'Par's body and called forth trees to bind Mango, ultimately allowing Psyber to deliver the fatal blow.
