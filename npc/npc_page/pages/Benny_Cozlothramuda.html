本名班傑明，出身於歷史悠久的矮人家族。沃瓦倫長年的腕力冠軍，個性爽朗。曾作為潔絲敏失蹤事件的搜救隊一員，雖然對於沒能找到潔絲敏感到懊惱，但沒有過度責怪自己。慶祝葛蘭倫回到沃瓦倫的派對上，班尼與烏西歐尼比腕力，輸後大方把刻有家族姓氏的金戒指送給他。沃瓦倫滅鎮事件中，班尼死亡並被化為殭屍，最後由冒險者們讓他安息。

Born Benjamin, from an old dwarven family.Benny was Vowalon's long-time arm-wrestling champion and had a cheerful personality. He joined the search party for the missing Jasmine, and though he regretted failing to find her, he did not blame himself too harshly.At the party celebrating Grenland's return to Vowalon, Benny arm-wrestled Usioni and, after losing, generously gave him a gold ring engraved with his family name. During the Vowalon massacre, Benny died and was turned into a zombie, and the adventurers ultimately laid him to rest.
