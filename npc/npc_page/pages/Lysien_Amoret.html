覆羽莊園的主人，周遭時間流動異常，與羽毛、天使和岡斯身上的詛咒有關。他曾視岡斯為實驗失敗品，並稱他的變化與芬尼爾王冠有關。冒險者介入後，利希恩透過儀式被推向芬尼爾的神性，最終成為「永恆與愛之神阿莫雷」；覆羽莊園消失，原址留下供奉他的新神殿。

A son of the Amoret family in Baker and master of the Feathered Estate, where time behaved strangely. His condition was tied to feathers, angels, and the curse affecting GoR.Gons considered him a failed experiment and linked his transformation to the Crown of Phyneal.After the adventurers intervened, Lysien was pushed through a ritual into the divinity of Phyneal and the Amoret line, becoming the Amoret God of Eternity and Love; the Feathered Estate vanished, leaving a new shrine to him in its place.
