Fre'Do

常翠林的信使，出身於世代侍奉巴斯家族的家系。約五十年前，弗雷多離開故鄉來到常翠林；他的父親支持巴斯的父親，並深入研究死靈術，而十分照顧他的哥哥因為做了更多研究，轉而反對父親。弗雷多也因此選擇了自己的道路。在常翠林總部，弗雷多曾帶巴斯探視休養中的賽柏；芒果襲擊後，他向巴斯透露派翠克的遺體遭人挖走，並提議帶冒險者前往石原鎮。前往石原鎮的路上，弗雷多帶著巴斯與烏波徒步避人耳目，提醒眾人提防狼人蹤跡，並以乾糧與守夜安排照料隊伍。他也協助調停烏波與冷火腿的緊張對峙，之後帶著一行人穿過雅洛斯，繼續朝石原鎮前進。

A courier of the Evergreen, Fre'Do comes from a family that has served Buzz's family for generations.About fifty years ago, Fre'Do left his homeland and came to the Evergreen. His father supported Buzz's father and pursued deep research into necromancy, while Fre'Do's elder brother, who had cared for him greatly, opposed their father after conducting further research. Fre'Do chose his own path as a result.At the Evergreen headquarters, Fre'Do led Buzz to visit the recuperating Psyber. After Mango's attack, he told Buzz that Patrick's body had been dug up and offered to guide the adventurers to Stonefield.On the road to Stonefield, Fre'Do led Buzz and Ubbo on foot to avoid notice, warned the group about signs of werewolves, and saw to their rations and night watches. He also helped defuse the tense confrontation between Ubbo and Cold Ham, then led the group through Yalos as they continued toward Stonefield.
