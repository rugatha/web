弗萊特境內的嚮導之一，相對年輕而較無經驗，但根據師父的說法「很有潛力」。事後冒險者們發現他是師父的孫子。嗜酒；在與冒險者們的旅行過程中，受到圖伊特感化而戒酒，並信奉芬尼爾。在母親大人異變事件後，佛拉與羅特、芒果成為僅存的三人。多年後，他身為戴著白色面具的芬尼爾信徒，與羅特的隊伍交戰；重傷昏迷前喊出羅特的名字，身分才被認出。羅特不願再失去這名舊夥伴，治療並一路照料他，帶著他跟隨派琶前往常翠林。即使仍未完全康復、行走跛腳，佛拉仍主動分擔物資，並在芒果與蛇妖襲擊常翠林總部時支援冒險者，於危急時刻治療眾人。他認為身為芬尼爾信徒，不適合久留在涅西斯的領地；與羅特談過母親大人事件留下的傷痛後，兩人一同悄悄離開常翠林，尋找尚未決定的未來。

One of the guides in Phreit, Phola was younger and less experienced than the others, but Shifu described him as promising. The adventurers later learned that he was Shifu's grandson.Once addicted to alcohol, he was influenced by Tweeter during his travels with the adventurers to quit drinking and become a follower of Phyneal.After the massacre committed by The Mother in her mutated form, Phola, Lott, and Mango were the only survivors.Years later, Phola fought Lott's party as a white-masked follower of Phyneal. He called Lott's name before collapsing from his wounds, revealing his identity. Unwilling to lose another old companion, Lott healed and cared for him while the group followed Py'Par to the Evergreen.Though still limping and not fully recovered, Phola helped carry supplies and supported the adventurers during Mango and the Lamiae's attack on the Evergreen headquarters, healing others at critical moments.Believing that a follower of Phyneal could not remain long in Nessis's domain, Phola spoke with Lott about the pain left by The Mother's massacre. The two then quietly left the Evergreen together to seek an as-yet undecided future.
