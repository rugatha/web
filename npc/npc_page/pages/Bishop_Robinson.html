An archbishop of the Church of the Keinra.During the St. Rayami incident, Robinson received Captain Marin's report in Fanaur and compared the island's disappearances and poisonings with the Lababi stones beneath Nimu Orphanage. He assigned inquisitors to investigate the orphanage. After Midori found records showing that the orphanage had sold children, she confronted and attacked Robinson, dropped her holy symbol, and escaped; Robinson was injured but still asked the adventurers to find Midori, calm her, and return the symbol.
